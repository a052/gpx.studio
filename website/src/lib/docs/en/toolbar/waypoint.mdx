[Waypoints](../gpx) can be added to GPX files to mark locations of interest on the map and display them on your GPS device.

### Creating a waypoint

To create a waypoint, fill in the form shown below.
You can choose the location of the waypoint either by clicking on the map or by entering the coordinates manually.
Validate the form when you are done.

<div class="flex flex-row justify-center">
    <Waypoint class="text-foreground p-3 border rounded-md shadow-lg" />
</div>

### Editing a waypoint

The form above can also be used to edit an existing waypoint after selecting it on the map.
If you only need to move the waypoint, you can drag it to the desired location.
